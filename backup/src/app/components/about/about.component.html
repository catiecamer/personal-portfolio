I’m fascinated with turning problems into possibilities💡

From balancing the books...

I started my career as a CPA, crunching numbers 📈. As I worked my way up the ladder, I realized that I was losing "interest" (pun intended) in accounting. I decided to take a leap of faith and explore a career that involves both creativity and critical thinking. After some self-discovery, I made the switch to product design and made sure to bring my problem-solving skills with me. I use these skills to identify user pain points, develop creative solutions, and design effective user experiences.

And painting canvases...

By the way, I also paint abstract pieces 🎨 and have been selling my art for about three years now. Although it's not directly related to my work as a product designer, I find that my artistic background has been helpful in bringing over some visual design principles. I'm always looking for ways to bring a touch of creativity to my work and keep things fresh.

To designing user experiences!

I fell in love with UX during my immersive course at General Assembly 🎓. After that, I took on contract work, gaining hands-on experience across different industries. At FundsDLT, I stepped into the complex world of fund distribution and focused on making processes clearer and more user-friendly. I conducted research, improved the design system, led heuristic analyses, and facilitated workshops to drive innovation. I take a thoughtful, research-driven approach to problem-solving and am always eager to create seamless, intuitive experiences.
