Who is FundsDLT?

FundsDLT offers a SaaS blockchain-based service designed to simplify and digitize fund distribution operations. The platform aims to create a transparent ecosystem for all participants in fund distribution, including investors, asset managers, distributors, custodians, and transfer agents.

Currently, FundsDLT has onboarded four large customers with its asset manager and transfer agent platforms.

Error feedback examples

Users get frustrated when they are not able to complete their tasks due to errors that they don't understand. They feel stuck as they always have to contact FundsDLT when errors occur.

Solution and action plan

  • By highlighting user feedback, the priority of improving error messages has been elevated within FundsDLT
  • Readability: As a first step it was recommended that error messages are clear, concise, and free of technical jargon
  • Actionable Feedback: As a second step, we need to include specific steps users can take to resolve the issue
  • Iterative Improvements: Lastly, we need to continuously refine error messages based on user feedback and testing results

Distributor platform

The Distributor platform would be aimed at Distributors whom act as intermediaries between asset management companies and investors. FundsDLT would help distributors by providing a blockchain-based solution that streamlines onboarding trade placement, and fun management.

I was a part of the discovery phase for this new product. My role in these projects was more research-based, involving:

  • Persona
  • Problem definition
  • Prioritization
  • Extensive collaboration with the tech team and business analysts
  • User flow
  • Wireframing & testing

Persona

I began by creating a persona based on secondary research and conversations,as I did not have direct access to users for conducting user research. This persona helped me understand the motivations and frustrations of potential users, which guided the subsequent steps.

The problem

Distributors needed a centralized system for investor onboarding (including KYC and screening), fund information access, order placement, and reporting. These tasks were previously spread across various systems, some still paper-based, causing inefficiencies, higher error rates, and poor investor services, which could lead to client loss.

Prioritization

To determine the must-haves, I created a MoSCow table. This helped in diverging on all the information and identifying the minimum viable product (MVP). I validated the must-haves internally with management before proceeding to the nest steps.

The scope was limited to must-have items differed widely from Vision (Transfer agent platform).

  1. Distributor universes (groups of funds that each distributor will be able to access)
  2. Distributor profiles with hierarchies
  3. Custody accounts at the investor and distributor layer

Technical feasibility

I collaborated extensively with business analysts and the tech team to understand the feasibility of solutions from a technical perspective.

  • Mapped out the order cycle
  • Determined the key information needed from each player to track down orders
  • Identified limitations which helped guide the first version of the design

User flow

With a clear understanding of the problem, I created a detailed user flow to map the entire process from the distributor's perspective. This user flow outlined each step that a distributor would take within the platform, from onboarding sub-distributors, creating fund universes at each distributor level and onboarding investors

By creating a comprehensive user flow, we were able to visualize the entire user journey, identify any gaps or inefficiencies, and ensure a seamless and efficient experience for the distributors. This step was essential in laying the groundwork for the subsequent wire-framing and high-fidelity mockup stages.

Wireframing

I then developed wireframes, which I continuously validated with multiple team members to ensure alignment and address any issues early in the design process.

High-fidelity prototyping

Finally, I created high-fidelity mockups, which are now ready to be tested with the client. These mockups are designed to provide a seamless and efficient user experience, addressing the identified pain points and improving overall service delivery.
